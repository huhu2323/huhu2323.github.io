# After almost six years, we’re finally expecting! 

To be honest, I still can’t believe it. I’ve had this long-standing vision of just me and my wife living life on our own—traveling, exploring, and enjoying our freedom. We never planned for this. With the economy the way it is and the high cost of raising a child, it feels daunting. We never felt the need for a child to feel 'complete'; we were perfectly happy as just the two of us.

Here in the Philippines, there’s a strong cultural belief that a family isn’t 'real' without a child. Kids are often seen as the 'glue' that holds a couple together. I’ve always felt that’s a wrong mindset. If a couple stays together *only* for the sake of raising a child, it suggests they aren’t truly happy with each other.

That said, if you do bring a child into the world, you must raise them with everything you have. Show them love and let them enjoy life without pressure. I grew up with the mindset that I had to carry my family once I got a career—that finding a job meant immediately taking on the responsibility of supporting my parents. That is a burden I never want my son to carry. I want him to enjoy life and pursue what he loves.

I am so grateful for this son, but I admit, I’m nervous. I worry that I might not be able to raise him carefully enough. But I know that with love and intention, we can break that cycle.
